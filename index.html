Lodha Whitefield Project Overview

Lodha Whitefield is an upcoming ultra-luxury residential development by Lodha Group at Hope Farm Junction in Whitefield, Bangalore. Planned across approximately 9 acres, the project is expected to feature 6 premium high-rise towers with spacious 3, 3.5 & 4 BHK residences starting from approximately 2,500 sq. ft.

The project is positioned opposite Mahindra Blossom and adjacent to Alembic City, within Whitefield’s established residential and IT corridor. A major location advantage is its proximity to Hopefarm Channasandra Metro Station on the Purple Line, providing metro connectivity to other parts of Bangalore.

Currently in the pre-launch stage, the development is planned with a grand clubhouse, landscaped precincts and a range of lifestyle amenities. The supplied reference also identifies the project as an upcoming Lodha Group development at Hope Farm, Whitefield, with six residential towers and larger-format residences.

Lodha Whitefield Specifications

Lodha Whitefield Specifications

Lodha Whitefield is planned as an ultra-luxury residential development with spacious residences, premium finishes and thoughtfully designed infrastructure. The project is planned with modern residential specifications covering structure, flooring, doors, plumbing, electrical systems, security and vertical transportation.

Structure

RCC-framed structure with concrete solid block masonry for internal and external walls.

Flooring

Marble flooring is planned for living rooms, dining areas, foyer and bedrooms, with anti-skid ceramic tiles in bathrooms and utility areas.

Doors

Engineered main door frames with veneer-finished shutters and quality hardware. Bathroom doors are planned with veneer finish externally and laminated finish internally.

Plumbing & Sanitary

Premium CP fittings and sanitary fixtures from reputed brands such as Kohler, Toto or equivalent, along with rainwater harvesting provisions.

Security

Round-the-clock security with intercom facilities and CCTV surveillance at key vantage points.

Electrical

3-phase BESCOM power supply with concealed copper wiring and premium modular switches from reputed brands.

Power Backup

100% DG backup is planned for common areas and facilities, with provisional backup provisions for residences.

Elevators

Automatic passenger lifts with emergency communication facilities and DG power backup.
